The full Forum Ventures browser audit has now completed, including its public application landing pages, funding programs, investor network, legal terms and talent intake.

Three findings materially change the PlatFormula strategy.

1\. The $250K is not actually available on day one of applying. Forum says the investment occurs at company formation, after validation and investment committee approval. Founders retain majority ownership and board control, but the Studio's exact equity split and IP ownership terms are not publicly disclosed. [AI Studio terms](https://www.forumvc.com/ai-studio?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

2\. Forum has four distinct entry points, not one universal application.

| Program        | Published offer                                         | Application |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| AI Studio      | $250K upon approved formation                           | Apply       |
| Foundry        | Free five-week validation sprint                        | Apply       |
| Accelerator    | $100K for 7.5% via post-money SAFE                      | Pitch Forum |
| Talent Network | Potential venture-building and first-hire opportunities | Join        |

The Accelerator targets existing B2B companies with an MVP or near-MVP and less than $500K previously raised. [Accelerator details](https://www.forumvc.com/accelerator?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

3\. White-labeling is not currently authorized. Forum's [Terms of Use](https://www.forumvc.com/terms-of-use?utm_source=chatgpt.com) restrict commercial exploitation and grant no implied license. No public API, reseller agreement or referral compensation arrangement was found.

There is an additional IP concern: Forum's terms describe submitted feedback as non-confidential and non-proprietary. That makes a patent-attorney review and careful control of invention disclosures important before sharing proprietary architecture.

### What I would change in V3 immediately

Add a Forum Program Router that matches founders to the Studio, Foundry, Accelerator or investment fund based on their actual stage. Add a separate Talent Network pathway. Keep the Venture Record and evidence inside PlatFormula until the founder approves a specific disclosure.

Also add Forum's Industry Council model to our investor-network strategy. Forum operates invite-only councils connecting industry decision-makers with startups for feedback, introductions and potential pilots. That offers a more practical customer-acquisition model than waiting for investors to join a new marketplace. [Forum Industry Councils](https://www.forumvc.com/forum-industry-council?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

One final diligence issue: Forum's public pages contain inconsistent portfolio counts, follow-on funding percentages and pre-seed investment ranges. Those should be flagged as source discrepancies, not copied into PlatFormula marketing.

Bottom line: Forum is a credible potential downstream partner and a useful operational benchmark. It is not currently a licensed white-label backend. Build the independent routing and qualification layer first, then negotiate access to its network.
