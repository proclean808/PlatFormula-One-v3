# Forum Ventures reference — independent PlatFormula.One routing

Reviewed November 18, 2026. Forum is a comparator and external program resource, not a PlatFormula.One partner, licensed backend or source of guaranteed capital.

Forum's AI Studio targets domain experts building AI-native B2B companies from concept toward a first raise. Its investment is made at formation following validation and investment-committee approval, not upon submitting an application. Its Accelerator targets existing early-stage B2B software companies with an MVP or near-MVP and less than $500K raised; the current published terms are $100K for 7.5% via a post-money SAFE. These are Forum's published program terms, never PlatFormula.One offers or guarantees. [1] [2]

The Foundry is a free five-week, remote validation sprint. The page's listed application deadline is November 4, 2026 and its cohort start is November 9. Both dates precede this review; do not present this cohort as currently open merely because the page retains Apply links. Future cohort availability requires checking the provider. [3]

The official routes are AI Studio, Foundry, Accelerator and Pre-Seed Fund, with a separate Talent Network. Use stage and goal selections for local, rule-based routing. Do not automatically forward the founder's venture brief, patent details or files into external forms. Opening a destination is not an application submission. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Forum's terms grant no implied IP license and restrict commercial exploitation. They treat website feedback as non-confidential/non-proprietary and tell visitors not to submit confidential ideas as feedback. This is a disclosure warning, not a legal determination of how every separate application or negotiated agreement treats information. Have qualified counsel review sensitive disclosures and actual agreements. No Forum branding, logos, testimonials, portfolio counts or funding-rate claims should be copied into PlatFormula.One marketing. [5]

Industry Councils are a useful model for domain-expert feedback, customer discovery and potential pilots. Forum's page describes curated onboarding and private introductions; some sector routes are Coming Soon. Do not imply access to those people or pilots is available through PlatFormula.One. [6]

## Verified pre-seed criteria and practical council strategy

The official Pre-Seed Fund FAQ targets B2B founders with an MVP in market, meaningful early traction and domain expertise. It describes typical fit as at least $500K previously raised or $250K+ ARR; these are stage guides, not automatic approval criteria. Its hero says up to $1M while its FAQ gives a $250K–$750K range. Omit those discrepant check-size claims from public routing. [7]

For PlatFormula.One, the independent customer-council strategy is to identify domain reviewers, record founder-authorized conversations, ask for specific product feedback and seek a narrowly scoped pilot before making a fundraising claim. No Forum executive, investor, customer introduction or pilot is promised. Any outreach, disclosure or external contact still requires the founder's approval; this release does not automate it.
