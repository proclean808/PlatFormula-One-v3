### PlatFormula.One V3 — Pricing Revision & Product Expansion

November 18, 2026 · Commercial architecture update

The product needs to move beyond application automation. The stronger proposition is a venture-building environment where founders can develop a company, validate it, launch it, attract customers and prepare for investment.

Applications and pitch decks are deliverables. They should not define the entire platform.

### 1\. Revised pricing

Annual subscriptions receive 20% off the monthly rate. The Founder tier becomes $20/month, with an annual equivalent of $16/month. To preserve the intended $15.99 price exactly, that tier needs a slightly greater discount (20.05%).

| Tier        | Monthly billing | Annual billing      | Annual savings |
| ----------- | --------------- | ------------------- | -------------- |
| Explore     | 5-day trial     | —                   | —              |

| Growth      | $49             | $470.40 ($39.20/mo) | $117.60        |

## 3. Investor Pressure Chamber

Build a scenario-based rehearsal environment.

Support skeptical VC, technical investor, financial investor and enterprise buyer personas.

Support adjustable difficulty, live voice, contextual interruptions, deck review and challenging follow-up questions.

Score answers against founder-approved venture facts, evidence quality, clarity, defensibility and handling of objections.

Do not fabricate financial performance or customer traction.

Begin with a functioning text-based simulator. Add real-time voice and screen-aware evaluation only after verifying the underlying integrations.

## 4. Product architecture

Every module reads and updates the same canonical Venture Record.

Joyce is the persistent user-facing coordinator.

ConductorCLAW manages agent orchestration.

Logic Lattice authorizes consequential actions.

Grounding Fabric supplies source-backed context.

Evidence Ledger records outcomes and verification.

Use a reusable Lattice adapter system for LinkedIn Genius and future partner integrations.

Do not duplicate founder information across disconnected tools.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

Do not mark modules complete based on mockups.

For every module, deliver:
- Functional user journey
- Server-side entitlement enforcement
- Persistent data
- Usage accounting
- Error handling
- Evidence of successful execution
- Mobile acceptance testing
- Explicit IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED / PLANNED / BLOCKED status

Preserve legacy intellectual property, but develop against PlatFormula-One-v3 exclusively.

**Product objective:** A founder should be able to enter with an idea and progressively leave with a validated business hypothesis, functioning product, launch assets, investor materials and a verifiable venture record.

The strongest commercial differentiator is StitchPitch Live connected to an actual working venture, with Joyce guiding the founder and the Evidence Ledger proving what has been accomplished. That combines capabilities the researched competitors generally offer separately.

I would prioritize that integrated experience over a standalone public-speaking tool, a large template library or an elaborate 3D homepage.
