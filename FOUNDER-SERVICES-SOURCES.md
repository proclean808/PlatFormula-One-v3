# Founder services and presentation references

Verified November 18, 2026. Each resource was independently checked against its official product or government site; Cornell LII is explicitly a third-party directory. Public cards use original short descriptions and **External Resource** status. A working destination is not proof of professional credentials, eligibility, price, quality, affiliation or a connected integration.

Corgi's startup-insurance page supports insurance discovery, not a coverage recommendation. Stripe Atlas and Clerky describe company setup and legal paperwork while disclaiming individualized legal services. Mercury describes itself as a fintech using partner banks. Carta Launch supports early-stage equity management; Cooley GO offers public legal document resources. Their live terms must be reviewed by founders before use. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

The USPTO link opens the actual practitioner search. USPTO does not recommend a particular practitioner. Cornell's Sacramento listing is useful for discovery, but does not verify credentials, suitability or availability. No local firm ratings or unverified referral relationships were copied from the addendum. [7] [8] [9]

Yoodli, PitchBob and Papermark are external rehearsal, presentation and document-sharing references respectively, not live PlatFormula integrations. Perspective AI's verified destination is `getperspective.ai`, not the similarly named parked site. Outset and Strella provide customer-research references for Validate. [10] [11] [12] [13] [14] [15]
