### YC Founder Stack — The Companies Behind the Startups

The names you're recalling are probably Brex and Ramp. Both help startups manage corporate spending, cards, expense controls and financial operations.

The Brazilian-founder clue points particularly strongly toward Brex. Its cofounders, Henrique Dubugras and Pedro Franceschi, are Brazilian entrepreneurs, and Brex itself went through Y Combinator's Winter 2017 batch.

Brex, Ramp and Mercury are three of the most recognizable names in the startup financial stack, but they serve overlapping rather than identical functions.

| Company | Core function                                         | Why YC founders use it                  |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Brex    | Corporate cards, expenses, travel, financial controls | Managing startup spending after funding |
| Ramp    | Cards, expenses, procurement, bill payments           | Controlling burn and automating finance |
| Mercury | Business banking, payments, treasury                  | Operating accounts and managing cash    |
| Rho     | Banking, spend management and treasury                | Consolidating financial operations      |
| Carta   | Cap tables, SAFEs, equity administration              | Managing ownership and fundraising      |

The practical distinction is that Mercury manages the company's money, while Brex and Ramp help manage how it gets spent. Founders don't necessarily need all three.
