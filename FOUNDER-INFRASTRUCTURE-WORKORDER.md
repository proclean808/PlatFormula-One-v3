### 1\. Patent & Intellectual Property Studio

The system should recognize when a founder describes potentially protectable technology and offer an IP assessment.

It should distinguish between patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets and contractual ownership. Not every startup needs a patent.

Proposed founder experience

* Founder describes an invention, technical architecture, algorithm or product.

* AI flags potential IP considerations, including public-disclosure timing, prior art, ownership and patentability questions.

* Founder receives a preliminary invention summary and research checklist, clearly marked as nonlegal analysis.

* The platform recommends consultation with a registered patent attorney or patent agent before filing or public disclosure.

* With permission, the founder exports an attorney-ready invention disclosure package.

A critical safeguard: a provisional patent application is not an examined patent, does not itself grant enforceable patent rights, and generally requires a corresponding nonprovisional filing within 12 months to preserve its benefit. The AI must never promise patentability or protection.

Patent counsel should be selected based on technical specialization, USPTO registration and relevant experience—not merely proximity.
